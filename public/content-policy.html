Learning material

IELTSist may present source-linked practice material, imported material, public-topic practice and official-style simulations. Source labels are part of the learning record. Use content only for the study or classroom purpose authorised for you, and do not treat an official-style simulation as an official examination.

Your uploads and responses

Only upload work that you are allowed to share. Do not include another person's private information, restricted test content, unlawful material or instructions intended to misuse the AI features. Keep a copy of important work outside the service.

AI-assisted feedback

AI-generated feedback is a study aid. It can identify a likely issue, evidence location or next practice action, but it may be wrong. Check substantive decisions against the source material and a qualified teacher where appropriate. A failed provider response must remain a failed response and is not a score.

Reporting an issue

For an access-code, classroom or content-rights issue, contact the school administrator or redemption-code issuer who provided access. This deployment does not expose a general public support or sales contact channel.
